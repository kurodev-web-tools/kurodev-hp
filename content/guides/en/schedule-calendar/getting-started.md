## Outcome

Keep a stream plan and its preparation status together so the next task is easier to identify.

## Prerequisites

- Choose a sample stream title, date, and time.

## Steps

1. Open Schedule Calendar.
2. Add a new plan with the sample date, time, and a short title.
3. Review the preparation status and save the plan.

![Schedule Calendar month view showing sample stream plans](/images/guide/schedule-calendar.png)

## Common problems

- If you are unsure how much to enter, start with a short title and the date and time, then add details later.
