## Outcome

Separate your stream preparation into planning, announcement images, and social posting, then open the tool that matches your next task.

## Prerequisites

- Decide on one preparation task you want to complete next.

## Steps

1. Open the [Schedule Calendar guide](/en/guide/schedule-calendar/getting-started) to organize a stream plan.
2. Open the [Thumbnail Editor guide](/en/guide/thumbnail-editor/getting-started) to make an announcement image.
3. Open the [SNS Split Image Maker guide](/en/guide/sns-split-image-maker/getting-started) to prepare a split-image post.

![Sample Kuro Stream Kit screen introducing three publication-verified tools](/images/guide/kuro-stream-kit-overview.png)

## Common problems

- If you are unsure where to start, choose the first unfinished task in your next stream preparation workflow.
