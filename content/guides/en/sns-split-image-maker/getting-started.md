## Outcome

Split one image into two, three, or four social-post images and check how they will be arranged.

## Prerequisites

- Prepare a publication-safe sample image.

## Steps

1. Open SNS Split Image Maker.
2. Choose the sample image and select the number of splits.
3. Review the preview and save order before saving the images.

![SNS Split Image Maker previewing the order of a split sample image](/images/guide/sns-split-image-maker.png)

## Common problems

- If the joined image becomes difficult to understand, use an image where important text or faces do not cross the split boundaries.
