## Outcome

Arrange text and visual elements into a stream announcement image and review it before export.

## Prerequisites

- Prepare a sample stream name and date to place in the image.

## Steps

1. Open Thumbnail Editor and choose a preset and canvas size.
2. Add text or shapes, arranging the most important information first.
3. Review the canvas and choose the required export format.

![Thumbnail Editor canvas with a sample stream announcement image](/images/guide/thumbnail-editor.png)

## Common problems

- If the priority text is difficult to read, check its contrast against the background before adding smaller details.
