## Outcome

Confirm that Comment Translator is not yet generally available and that no public launch action is provided.

## Prerequisites

- This guide explains availability and does not provide connection or setup instructions.

## Steps

1. Confirm that the current status is in development.
2. Confirm that no public launch action is displayed.
3. Use another publication-verified tool until the status changes.

![Availability screen showing that Comment Translator cannot be publicly launched yet](/images/guide/comment-translator-availability.png)

## Common problems

- If the tool cannot be opened, do not guess a connection destination or setup procedure.
